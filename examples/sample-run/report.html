Ablation — does the subject earn its tokens?

How to read this table. With and without are the primary numbers; the delta is derived from them and must never be read on its own.
With — the only arm your edit moves.
Compare it against the same cell in your previous run. This is where a real change shows up.
Without — the base model, and it drifts.
It moves between runs for reasons that have nothing to do with your edit. A delta that changed sign usually means this arm moved: a subject going from 100/80 = +20% to 100/100 = ±0% while its with-arm never budged is not a regression.
Delta — derived, and ambiguous by construction.
Before calling a delta change a regression or a win, look at which arm actually moved.
